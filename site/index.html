Good to know: definitions and analogies may vary. These notes are simplified, beginner-friendly explanations, and quiz questions can be worded differently or go deeper. Please do in-depth research on each service listed here — the official AWS documentation is the best place to start.
EC2

EC2

Elastic Compute Cloud
rent-a-computer, cloud edition
like PUBG

A computer you rent in the cloud instead of buying one. Switch it on, run anything, pay only while it's on.

The PUBG match runs on a powerful rented computer online, not on your phone. That rented computer is EC2.

S3

S3

Simple Storage Service
your giant online storage locker
like Google Drive

A giant online storage locker for your files. Upload once, grab it back anytime, from anywhere.

Teammates open files from one shared Drive folder. S3 is that Drive, built for your app's files.

VPC

VPC

Virtual Private Cloud
a private room at a very public party
like an Incognito tab

Your own private, fenced-off space inside the cloud. You decide exactly who and what is allowed in.

An Incognito tab is a private bubble for your session. A VPC is a private bubble for your app.

IAM

IAM

Identity & Access Management
the guest list at the door
like a permission letter

The guard who checks who's allowed to do what. Nobody gets more access than they need.

No signed letter, no exit. IAM decides who gets which letter, for which door.

λ

Lambda

AWS Lambda
a doorbell that only rings when pressed
like a field trip

Code that wakes up only when needed. Does one small job, then goes back to sleep.

A field trip happens only on trip day. Lambda runs your code only when triggered, and you pay for that run.

RDS

RDS

Relational Database Service
a notebook someone else maintains for you
like lecture notes

A shared notebook that holds your app's data. Someone else handles backups and upkeep.

Organised notes plus a spare copy. RDS is an organised notebook for your data, backups included.

CW

CloudWatch

Amazon CloudWatch
eyes (and a pulse check) on everything
like a fitband

A fitness tracker, but for your app. Watches its pulse and buzzes you if something's off.

A fitband buzzes when something looks wrong. CloudWatch watches your app's numbers and alerts you.

53

Route 53

Amazon Route 53
the signpost for your website
like a bus driver

The reception desk for your website. Points every visitor straight to the right place.

Tell the driver your stop and he takes the right route. Route 53 sends myapp.com visitors to the right server.

AS

Auto Scaling

EC2 Auto Scaling
extra hands appear exactly when needed
like canteen puffs

More help shows up the moment things get busy, and quietly leaves once it's quiet.

The canteen opens extra counters at the rush. Auto Scaling adds servers when traffic jumps and removes them when it's quiet.

    Guidelines

    Rules & FAQ

    Please read these before Round 1.

    RuleNo AI tools during the quiz

    The quiz is online and strictly no-AI. Using ChatGPT, Claude or any AI tool is prohibited.

    RuleOne writer per team

    Only one nominated roll number per team may attempt the quiz.

    RuleOne attempt only

    If more than one member of a team attempts the quiz, the whole team is disqualified.

    Do I need AWS experience?

    No. The quiz is made for beginners.

    How big can a team be?

    Teams can have 1 or 2 members, for both rounds.

    Do all participants receive something?

    Yes. Participation certificates will be provided for all participants.
